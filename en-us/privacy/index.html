Privacy policy

This policy covers the Vorlinx macOS application and explains how personal information is handled in the app. Vorlinx is designed around local management: private files and AI processing stay on your device. We do not operate backend servers to receive, store or analyze that private material.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or upload the contents or names of your files, reading history, notes, searches, voice recordings, reference voices, model inputs or generated results. We do not send your app usage to ourselves for advertising, tracking or profiling. We do not sell or rent your private information, provide it to advertisers or use it to train remote AI models.

2. Local files, AI and speech processing

File organization, semantic indexing, natural language search, image analysis, speech recognition and read aloud are processed on your device. Indexes, reference audio, voice data, reading progress and app data are stored locally and are not uploaded to us when you use these features. Local diagnostic and cache files are not automatically sent to us either.

3. Permissions remain under your control

File access, microphone, speech recognition and local network permissions are used for the corresponding features you choose to use. You can manage or revoke permissions in macOS System Settings; features that depend on a revoked permission may no longer work. Only use files and reference voices you are entitled to use.

4. Model preparation and network actions

Preparing models or language resources for the first time may connect to your selected model source or system resource service. Subsequent analysis of private material and AI inference run locally; your files, recordings and model inputs are not sent to those download sources. External websites you choose to open handle requests under their own policies.

5. Local network import and sharing

You enable cross-device import and sharing. Files transfer between devices and recipients over the local network, without relay servers operated by us. You can set a sharing password and expiry and stop sharing. Give links or QR codes only to people you trust; stopping a share does not delete copies that recipients have already downloaded.

6. Encrypt before backing up

Backups generated by Vorlinx are encrypted before being saved to your chosen folder. If iCloud or another service syncs that folder, it syncs the encrypted backup data. We do not receive your backups or hold the keys or recovery information on your behalf. Keep the information needed for recovery safe. This encryption description applies to backups; it does not mean shared files or all original local files are automatically encrypted.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your devices and in backup locations you choose, for as long as you decide. Use app controls or system file management tools to remove data, models and backups you no longer need. Uninstalling the app may not remove original files, external folders or existing backups. Manage copies and trash in any sync service you use. We do not hold server copies of this private material that you need to ask us to delete.

8. Contact and policy updates

If you email us, the address, issue description and attachments you choose to provide are used to handle your request. Do not send unnecessary private files. A contact email will be added here and on the support page. If future releases change data handling, we will update this policy and explain changes or seek permission where needed. Direct multi-cloud backup is currently planned; relevant details will be added when it becomes available.

Keep everyday files in view and give your ideas room to grow.
